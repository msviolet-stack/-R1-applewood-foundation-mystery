The Missing Artifact

Founders’ Night is hours away. The Foundation’s treasured Golden Apple has vanished from its locked gallery case. No broken glass. No alarm. Five people were inside.

Case Desk

At 4:15 PM, staff discovered that the Golden Apple was missing from its locked display case. The gallery alarm had not sounded.

Your task: Identify who removed the artifact, explain how it was done, and support your accusation with evidence. Do not accuse someone because of one suspicious clue.

Known facts

The Golden Apple is 28 cm wide. The gallery has a staff door controlled by access cards. A service corridor connects the gallery to the basement maintenance area. The gallery camera experienced a short interruption during scheduled system work.

Display Case

The glass is undamaged. The case was unlocked using its normal mechanism.

Pedestal Floor

A thin streak of red-brown dust is beside the pedestal. The public gallery floor itself is pale stone.

Object Size

The Golden Apple is 28 cm wide and fairly heavy. Moving it unnoticed would be easier with a container, cloth, trolley, or bag.

Camera

The gallery camera freezes for 62 seconds at 4:04 PM. The interruption is recorded as scheduled maintenance.

Teacher Case Solution

Answer: Leo Grant. Ella is a credible alternative lead because she enters the gallery and carries a large bag; Nora has an apparent safety motive; Samir's role makes the camera interruption look suspicious.

Leo had legitimate control of the alarm maintenance window, entered during that window, and was seen bringing a trolley and protective cloth toward the gallery. Most importantly, the dust beside the pedestal matches the maintenance workshop and appears on the trolley wheel. The intended reconstruction is that Leo used the maintenance test to avoid triggering the alarm, moved the artifact using the trolley, and hid it temporarily because he believed the crowded event put it at risk.

Discussion point: Opportunity or motive alone is not proof. The strongest conclusion is supported by multiple independent clues.
